Supporting & Donating to AcreetionOS

Distributions have real bills: mirrors, ISO hosting bandwidth, build hardware, domain/DNS, coffee maintaining keyrings at 2 AM.

Where money goes

Cost center Why it matters

ISO/mirror bandwidth

Downloads are the product’s front door (us.iso server runs on this)

Build infrastructure

Clean-chroot builders per release cycle (Building the AcreetionOS ISO scale)

Domains & DNS

acreetionos.org family incl. this docs subdomain

Test hardware diversity

The laptop models QA doesn’t own personally

How to give

Donation channels are listed on the site’s donate page — kept current there rather than duplicated here (payment options churn faster than docs cycles). Recurring amounts beat one-shots for planning; infrastructure likes predictability.

Non-money currencies equally welcome

  • Time: any lane in contributing — testers and doc writers convert directly into project quality

  • Hardware: spare machines broaden the compatibility matrix (coordinate via community channels before shipping anything)

  • Advocacy: honest recommendations in your circles; comparison writeups grounded in facts

  • Professional skills: legal review, design passes, accessibility audits — offer via maintainer contact

Corporate sponsorship framing

Companies standardizing fleets on AcreetionOS: sponsored infra (mirrors in your region especially) benefits you directly while funding the commons — reach out through site contact for specifics.

Transparency norm

Aggregate donation usage gets reported through community channels periodically — donors deserve to see mirrors spin faster, not disappear into fog.
