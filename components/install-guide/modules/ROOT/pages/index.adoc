Welcome aboard. This guide covers every step from empty USB stick to logged-in desktop.

== The path at a glance

. xref:requirements.adoc[Check requirements] — 2 minutes
. xref:download.adoc[Download & verify] the ISO — 10 minutes of waiting
. xref:bootable-usb.adoc[Write it to USB] — 5 minutes
. xref:bios-uefi.adoc[Prepare firmware] if needed — usually nothing
. Boot the xref:live-environment.adoc[live session], try everything
. Run the xref:installer-walkthrough.adoc[installer] — ~10 minutes
. Reboot into your new system → xref:post-install.adoc[final touches]

== Time & risk budget

Installation itself erases only what you tell it to erase. Guided "Erase disk" wipes the chosen drive completely; *Manual* mode lets you carve space precisely — read xref:partitioning.adoc[partitioning] before choosing manual with existing data.

Snapshot-worthy moment comes later — after install, per xref:wiki::post-installation.adoc[first steps].
